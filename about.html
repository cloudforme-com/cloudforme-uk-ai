About Mark Lilleyman

Technology experience with a human point of view.

I help organisations make sensible decisions about AI, cloud and adoption—then turn those decisions into practical action people can understand and use.

Experience that connects the dots

From MSP delivery to practical AI adoption.

I bring more than 20 years of managed services experience together with Microsoft training, Azure architecture and hands-on infrastructure knowledge.

That background matters because AI does not arrive in isolation. It has to fit the way people work, the technology already in place, the data a business relies on and the controls it needs. I help leaders and teams see that whole picture without turning the conversation into an enterprise transformation exercise.

As a Microsoft Certified Trainer and Azure Certified Architect, I am comfortable moving between strategy, technical reality and the learning people need to change their everyday work. Whether I am supporting an SME directly or working alongside an MSP, the aim is the same: clear advice, useful outcomes and no unnecessary noise.
